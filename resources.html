Data, models, software, and analysis tools used in my research.

Data

  • XRAIN — 国土交通省の気象レーダー観測データweather-radar observations from MLIT, Japan

  • Disdrometer observations — 雨滴粒径分布の地上観測データground observations of drop size distributions

  • CLIGEN — WEPPで用いる確率的気象生成モデルstochastic weather generator used with WEPP
  • SWAT / ArcSWAT — 流域スケールの水文・物質動態モデルwatershed-scale hydrology and transport model
  • WEPP notes — 個人用・研究室向けの操作メモpractical notes for using WEPP

  • DISDRODB — 雨滴粒径分布データの標準化・解析standardization and analysis of disdrometer data

  • xarray · pandas · NumPy · Matplotlib — データ処理・統計解析・可視化data processing, statistical analysis, and visualization
  • Jupyter Notebook / JupyterLab · conda — 解析環境analysis environment

Research workflows

  • Rainfall observation → erosivity
    雨滴径分布から降雨運動エネルギーを評価し、土壌侵食との関係を解析。 Estimate rainfall kinetic energy from drop size distributions and analyze its relationship with soil erosion.
  • Weather radar → spatial rainfall properties
    二重偏波レーダーと地上観測を組み合わせ、侵食に関わる降雨特性を面的に推定。 Combine polarimetric radar and surface observations to estimate erosion-relevant rainfall properties spatially.
  • Climate data → CLIGEN → WEPP
    将来気候データから降雨特性を生成し、土壌侵食リスクの将来変化を評価。 Generate rainfall characteristics from future-climate data and assess changes in future soil-erosion risk.
